# Home — Page Override

## Role

The homepage is the trust and qualification layer, not a product manual.

## Visual decisions

- Keep the existing red/black/white Rede Software identity.
- Use the hero to establish enterprise/public-sector credibility before feature density.
- Use one dominant CTA and one low-emphasis secondary action.
- Prefer product/interface proof over decorative illustration.
- Keep red accents focused on actions, active states and product highlights.
- Let neutral surfaces carry most of the page.

## Layout decisions

- Hero: asymmetric 2-column desktop, stacked mobile.
- Keep the first viewport visually calm; avoid multiple competing cards around the headline.
- Use section-level whitespace to separate problem, proof and conversion areas.
- Product screenshots/mockups should have subtle framing rather than oversized glow effects.

## Interaction

- Hover/focus transitions: 150–220ms.
- No aggressive scale-up on cards.
- CTA hover may slightly deepen the red and raise the shadow.
- Keyboard focus remains visible.
